# Archetypes (future phase, synthetic people only)

A cohort of 24 consenting synthetic people (three types, 8 each) answers questions for 6 weeks; their first 4 days of readings give a label-free signature, grouped into 3 archetypes. New people are matched after 4 days and start from their archetype's fusion head. Control: one fusion head pooled over the whole cohort. All starts keep asking up to 1 question a day.
