# When to pulse (synthetic people; the effect of a pulse is an assumption)

20 synthetic people per seed, 5 seeds, 6 weeks, 3 evening readings a day. A pulse helps only when the person is tense: the pattern that suits them adds about 1.0 to recovery, the other about 0.2. Each false pulse makes the person turn pulses off with probability 5%. Same gates for every policy: bracelet worn, not moving, at most 3 a day, 90 minutes apart; 20% of eligible moments held out to measure the effect.

| When to pulse | Pulses per week | Pulses at tense moments | Pulses at excitement | Tense moments helped (pulse or accepted offer) | Extra recovery per tense moment | People who turned pulses off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body alone: heart rate above the person's usual, not moving | 0.15 | 47% | 51% | 3% | 0.02 | 0% |
| Generic reading says tense (today's reading, no questions) | 0.49 | 86% | 6% | 16% | 0.10 | 0% |
| SOUL-Calibrate decision, one fixed pattern (breathing) | 1.00 | 88% | 6% | 56% | 0.36 | 2% |
| SOUL-Calibrate decision, pattern learned per person | 1.00 | 86% | 5% | 57% | 0.41 | 6% |

Pattern learned per person after 6 weeks: the one that suits them in 72% of people (50% by chance); error of the measured effect against the true one 0.51 (true effect about 1.0).
