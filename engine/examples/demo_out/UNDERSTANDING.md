# Understanding layer (docs/30)

People drawn from open experience-sampling data: 462 people from five studies. In the real data, people with more negative affect tell their negative emotions apart less well (Spearman rho = -0.27). 'Needs the layer' = top quarter of negative affect or bottom quarter of differentiation: 42% of people. Effects of the layer are assumptions from published studies; 3 seeds, 8 weeks, reading scored on weeks 7-8.

## A person who does not know what they feel

The 25% of people with the lowest differentiation in the real data, against the others. Instead of asking for the name of the feeling: ask what it is like (pleasant or unpleasant, a lot of energy or little, with 'not sure' allowed), show their own body signals first, and weigh each answer by how much this person's answers can be trusted.

### Round 1 (effects as reported)

| How the system asks and learns | Answers right (hard / others) | 'Not sure' | Reading right, weeks 7-8 (hard / others) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask for the name (today) | 0.75 / 0.85 | 0% / 0% | 0.66 / 0.68 |
| + ask what it is like (pleasant? energy?) | 0.89 / 0.92 | 6% / 4% | 0.70 / 0.70 |
| + body first (signals shown before asking) | 0.92 / 0.94 | 4% / 2% | 0.70 / 0.70 |
| + weigh answers by reliability | 0.92 / 0.94 | 4% / 2% | 0.71 / 0.71 |
