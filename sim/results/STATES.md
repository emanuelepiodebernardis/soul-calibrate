# Four states or eight?

Synthetic people, 5 seeds, 60 people each; states on the valence x arousal circle with the same signal strength; 6 recurring situations, 4 readings a day; closed questions. Chance: 0.25 with 4 states, 0.125 with 8. 'Within one' = the reading is the true state or the state next to it on the circle (for example content instead of calm).
