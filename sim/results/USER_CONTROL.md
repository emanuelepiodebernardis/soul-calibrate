# The user in control

Synthetic people, 5 seeds, 60 people each; 6 recurring situations; 4 readings a day; a 7-day calibration week, then 5 test days without questions; questions at ambiguous moments, closed options.

## A. The user chooses how many questions and when

| Setting | Questions per person (week) | Accuracy, test days | Speaks | Accuracy when speaking | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No questions | 0.0 | 0.504 | 7% | 0.818 | 0.918 |
| 1 a day, any time | 6.0 | 0.863 | 72% | 0.938 | 0.930 |
| 1 a day, evenings only (readings 2-3) | 4.9 | 0.809 | 64% | 0.925 | 0.911 |
| 2 a day, any time | 8.3 | 0.910 | 81% | 0.955 | 0.948 |
| 2 a day, evenings only | 6.0 | 0.863 | 74% | 0.950 | 0.934 |
| as many as needed (up to 4 a day) | 8.9 | 0.920 | 84% | 0.961 | 0.954 |

## C. Wrong answers: plain memory vs consensus memory (2 questions a day)

Consensus memory: an answer that agrees with nearby answers gains weight; a lone contradiction loses weight instead of erasing what many answers agree on.
